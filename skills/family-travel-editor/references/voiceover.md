# Prepare and record personal voiceover

Use narration where confirmed memories or context add something the pictures and live conversation cannot supply. Distinguish the author's facts from proposed wording and unanswered questions. Avoid describing every visible action or covering an intact family exchange.

Prepare one recording packet for the requested scenes: exact draft text, current timeline position, a realistic timing estimate, surrounding picture/dialogue/music and the purpose of each passage. Pair uncertain memories with short source previews where useful. Mark optional candidates as optional rather than presenting them as approved extra lines. Keep recording instructions outside the spoken text.

Before recording in an editor, work in a saved timeline copy. Check the intended input device, destination track, recording folder, available storage and a short test take. A connected microphone or a newly created empty track does not establish a working recording path. Let the author control the take and stop when asked; do not claim recording began from setup alone.

Keep selected takes and alternatives separate. On insertion, preserve whole thoughts and natural speech boundaries, then recheck music coverage, stable speech beds, sync, entry/exit and surrounding action using [audio joins](audio-joins.md). Fit the edit to the accepted performance rather than stretching or synthesizing missing personal speech without a specific request.

Recording hardware, editor control and transcription/alignment tools are not bundled. If the current session cannot record, provide the packet and a clear route to recording with the user's available tools; preserve the draft and current state for resumption.
