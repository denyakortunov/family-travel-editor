# Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026. Project: Family Travel Editor, maintained by Denis Kortunov.

## Local workflow and information used

This package contains instructions, templates and local scripts. It has no developer-operated backend, telemetry, advertising or analytics. The bundled scripts do not transmit footage or project information to the developer.

At your direction, your AI client and local tools may process selected video and audio files, filenames and paths, technical metadata, file hashes, scene plans, narration, preferences and timecoded comments. You may choose to provide photo dates or location metadata and redacted travel details to reconstruct a trip. These inputs are used to catalogue media, plan the story, prepare edits and check exports. Do not provide passwords, API keys, identity documents, payment details or unrelated private correspondence.

## Recipients and optional services

Your AI client processes the content you give it under its own terms and privacy settings; local execution does not mean that every AI inference stays on your computer. The package itself does not connect your email, photo library or cloud accounts. If you authorize an external transcription, analysis, review or publishing service, the selected content may be sent to that service under its own policies. You can use the core planning workflow without those optional services and decline a particular transfer.

## Storage, retention and control

Local catalogues, plans, logs, preferences, timelines and exports remain in the locations you choose until you delete them. Removing the skill does not automatically delete your film workspace. The developer does not receive or retain these local files. You control which sources are selected and can stop processing, remove local outputs or choose not to share them. Retention and deletion of conversations, cloud backups and external uploads are controlled by the respective provider's settings and policies.

## Support

If you submit a public GitHub issue, its text, attachments and GitHub account identity are visible publicly and may be retained in the repository's issue history. Do not attach private footage, personal identifiers, credentials or unredacted paths. You can edit or delete content where GitHub permits, or contact the maintainer through an issue about removal. GitHub processes account and usage data under its own privacy policy.

Questions: [project support](https://github.com/denyakortunov/family-travel-editor/issues). Material changes to this policy will be recorded in repository history with an updated effective date.
