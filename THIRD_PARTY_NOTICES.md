Resolve, FFmpeg, Python, optional models and service integrations are not redistributed by this repository. The included example footage is synthetic, generated by the example script. No private family footage, travel records or voice recordings are bundled.

The current social cover, desktop/mobile workflow diagrams and edit-mark icon are original editable SVG artwork. Raster covers are exports of that vector artwork. The example filenames, ranges and timelines illustrate the workflow; they are not screenshots of an application or evidence of a completed native edit. They contain no photographs or AI-generated imagery.

Historical diagrams retain earlier AI-generated device illustrations; those are illustrative, not application screenshots. Document icons reuse source assets in `docs/icons/`. The photographic concept cover from v0.3.3 has been removed from the current package; earlier release archives retain their original contents.
